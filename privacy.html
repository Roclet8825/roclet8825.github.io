JARVIS Mark II privacy policy

JARVIS Mark II is private software used by one person, its owner, on hardware the owner controls. No one else can sign in or use it.

What Google data it uses

After the owner signs in with Google, the assistant reads the owner's own Gmail inbox and Google Calendar and, only when the owner approves each message, sends mail from the owner's account. It does not access anyone else's data.

Where the data goes

All data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is used only to show the owner their own mail and calendar and to send mail the owner has approved.

The assistant's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the assistant's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens on the owner's computer can be deleted at any time.

Contact

Use the support address shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
